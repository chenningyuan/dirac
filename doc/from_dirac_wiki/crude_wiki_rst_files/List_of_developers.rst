Main authors
============

Trond Saue
----------

`|image0| </dirac/index.php/Image:Saue.png>`_

**Trond Saue** `|image1| </dirac/index.php/Image:Norway.png>`_
 trond.saue``@``irsamc.ups-tlse.fr
`http://dirac.ups-tlse.fr/saue <http://dirac.ups-tlse.fr/saue>`_

Contributions: general structure, closed-shell Hartree-Fock, density
functional theory module, quaternion symmetry scheme, wave function
analysis (mulliken/projection/visualization), expectation values, linear
response module

Lucas Visscher
--------------

`|image2| </dirac/index.php/Image:Visscher06.jpg>`_

**Lucas Visscher**
`|image3| </dirac/index.php/Image:The_netherlands.png>`_
 visscher``@``chem.vu.nl
`http://www.chem.vu.nl/~visscher/ <http://www.chem.vu.nl/~visscher/>`_

Contributions: general structure, relativistic coupled cluster methods,
Lévy-Leblond equation, spinfree Dirac equation, 4-index transformation
module, direct CI module (from MOLFDIR)

Hans Jørgen Aa. Jensen
----------------------

`|image4| </dirac/index.php/Image:HJAaJ2_2008.JPG>`_

**Hans Jørgen Aa. Jensen**
`|image5| </dirac/index.php/Image:Denmark.png>`_
 hjj``@``ifk.sdu.dk
`http://www.sdu.dk/staff/hjj.aspx <http://www.sdu.dk/staff/hjj.aspx>`_

Contributions: general structure, quaternion symmetry scheme, linear
response module, quadratic response module, open-shell Hartree-Fock,
Kramers-restricted MCSCF, parallellization of CI, London orbitals NMR,
two-step X2C (BSSinf, DKHinf), administrator, webmaster

Work in progress: 4c ESR, London orbitals magnetic susceptibilities and
ESR

Note to Dirac authors
=====================

To Dirac authors: please add your coordinates. You know best your
contributions and the amount of personal data that you wish to share
here. Don't make it too easy for harvesting spambots. Click
`here </dirac/index.php/Special:Upload>`_ to upload your photo and flag.
I got the flags from
`here <http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallery_of_sovereign_state_flags>`_.
If you don't like to the have the flags, feel free to remove them. I
think they show nicely that DIRAC is quite an international project.
