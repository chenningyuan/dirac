---------------------

-  Add your talk/presentation to the program

   topics" so that we remember it and find time for it

   program for you and others
-  Yes, you can modify the sessions, times - this program is self
   organized

Discussion topics
-----------------

Please simply add more topics. We will find time for them. The earlier
we know, the easier it will be to schedule them.

-  Testing, code coverage, benchmark runs
-  AMFI + X2C
-  Efficiency & HPC: OpenMP/MPI, GPUs
-  Convergence (I will be happy to discuss my experiences and tricks
   that I have used, Paul; radovan: i think this will be very useful, we
   could use your presentation/our discussion as a starting point for a
   SCF convergence tutorial for users)
-  Dipole and Monopole transition probabilities between different states
   with different sets of spinors (Paul)
-  Obtaining the I(-M\_J) spinor wave functions from I(+M\_J) (Miro)
-  RECP and possible applications (YoungChoon)
-  Algorithms for general definition of active spaces and configurations
   for a CI. I have added code to the GOSCI routines in cigosc.F to
   allow a general difintion of sub-spaces with arbitary numbers of
   orbitals. I then define a set of numbers of electrons in each of
   these sub-spaces. There can be several of these sets of numbers. I am
   sure that this may be closely related to GAS CI's but I would be
   interested to know if there are differences. If people are
   interested, we could discuss such a definition of constrained
   cdonfigurations for a CI. (Paul Bagus)

Handy Tips
----------

-  Bus tickets

   -  regular bus tickets cost **DKK 19** for a single ride (including
      changing lines) and are valid for 1 hour.
   -  alternatively you can "rent" a bus card at the 7 eleven store at
      the train station (deposit DKK 50). Using this card the cost for a
      bus ride reduces to **DKK 10**.
   -  arriving by train you can use your train ticket for the bus and
      get a free ride (to the campground).
   -  bus routes (look along line 21 on page 3):
      `http://www.fynbus.dk/dwn5757 <http://www.fynbus.dk/dwn5757>`_

Meeting program
===============

Prologue: Sunday May 22
-----------------------

-  20:00 `Mongolian BBQ <http://www.djengis-khan.dk/>`_

Monday May 23
-------------

The state of affairs
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  09:00 -- 09:15 Hans Jørgen Aa. Jensen: Opening, discussion of meeting
   program
-  09:15 -- 09:20 Introduction round (everybody)
-  09:20 -- 11:30 Status of DIRAC developments together with a fresh
   coffee

   -  Odense (Hans Jørgen Aa. Jensen)
   -  Toulouse (Trond Saue+Timo Fleig)
   -  Amsterdam (Luuk Visscher)
   -  Tromsø (Michal Repisky and Radovan Bast)
   -  Heidelberg (Markus Pernpointner)
   -  Linköping (Patrick Norman)
   -  Banska Bystrica (Miro Ilias)

-  11.30 -- 13.00 Lunch and Collaborations

Science with DIRAC part 1
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  13.00 -- 13.20 Timo Fleig: General excitation rank Coupled Cluster
   for excited states
-  13.20 -- 13.30 Mickael Hubert: Two ways to get a Commutator-Based
   Coupled Cluster Jacobian.
-  13.30 -- 13.50 Lucas Visscher: General remarks on relativistic CC

Science with DIRAC part 2
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  14.00 -- 14.20 Stefan Knecht: On the isotopic field shift in
   rotational spectra: a story of misunderstandings?
-  14.20 -- 15.00 Young Choon Park: Relativistic Effective Core
   Potential and Applications

Collaborative work part 1
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  16.30 -- 18.00 Work on collaborative projects

Recreation
^^^^^^^^^^

-  18:00 Pizza at the University
-  20:00 Soccer match at the campground
-  21:30 Beer, campfire, and stories

Tuesday May 24
--------------

Science with DIRAC part 3
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  09:00 -- 09:30 Paul Bagus: Core-Level Spectroscopies including NEXAFS
   and XPS. (This could also become a topic for Discussion. I believe
   that Ulf has thoughts about this problem.)
-  09:30 -- 10:00 Andre Gomes: Some updates on DFT-in-DFT Embedding
-  10:00 -- 10:30 Sebastian Höfener: WFT-in-DFT embedding theory
-  11:30 -- 12:30 Group Picture, then Lunch

Scripting and DIRAC
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  12:45 -- 13:15 Christoph Jacob: The PyADF scripting framework (and
   its use with Dirac)
-  13:30 -- 14:00 Ulf Ekstrom: Structured output and post processing

Discussing the technical framework for DIRAC
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  14:00 -- 14:30 Showdown: Git vs. Subversion (moderated by Radovan
   Bast)
-  14:30 -- 15:00 Building DIRAC across platforms: CMake vs. configure
   (moderated by Radovan Bast)

DIRAC11 release preparation
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  15:00 -- 15:15 Radovan Bast: Release strategies - what we can learn
   from other software projects
-  15:15 -- 15:30 Features, to-do list, release date, timeline,
   milestones, release message (with list of essential bugfixes)

Collaborative work part 2
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  15.30 -- 16.30 Mapping out of task groups and overlapping efforts
-  16:30 -- 18:00 Collaborative work and/or specific task groups

Wednesday May 25
----------------

Science with DIRAC part 4
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  09.00 -- 09.30 Ulf Ekström: Lieb maximization and Newton optimization
   of molecular properties.
-  09.30 -- 10.00 Luis Alvarez-Thon: Magnetically induced ring current
   densities: applications to heavy-element systems
-  10.00 -- 10.30 Coffee break
-  10.30 -- 11.00 Elke Faßhauer: Science for DIRAC --- Relativistic
   Lifetimes of Innervalent Ionized Systems
-  11.00 -- 11.20 Markus Pernpointner: Final state analysis in
   propagator methods, spin-orbit coupling constants in noble gas
   dimers.
-  11.45-- 12.30 Lunch

DIRAC on tomorrows computers
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  12.40 -- 13.00 Lucas Visscher: Our experiences with CUDA for GPU
   programming
-  13:00 -- 13:30 Miro Illias: Testing Dirac

DIRAC modularity
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  13.30 -- 14:00 Better modularity of basis set information (moderator:
   Trond)
-  14.00 -- 14.20 Cutting dead wood, removal of obsolete and untested
   features (moderator: Luuk)

Improved input and better defaults
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  14:30 -- 15:00 Discuss and collect a list of tasks for an improved
   input (user side, not input-reading itself)
-  15.00 -- 17:30 Task groups implement the list of improvements (more
   intuitive keywords, better defaults)

-  20.00 Conference dinner in Carlslund restaurant
   (`http://www.restaurant-carlslund.dk/ <http://www.restaurant-carlslund.dk/>`_)

Thursday May 26
---------------

Collaborative work part 3
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  09:00 -- 09:20 Hans Jørgen Aagaard Jensen: Hückel start guess
-  09:20 -- 10:00 Summary, assign tasks, etc. etc. (moderated by Hans
   Jørgen)
-  10:00 -- 12:00 Collaborative work
-  12:00 -- 13:00 Lunch
-  13:00 -- your time of departure: work on DIRAC and collaborative
   projects
