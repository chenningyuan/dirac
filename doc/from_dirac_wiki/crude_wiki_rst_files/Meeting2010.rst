DIRAC meeting 2010, Odense, May 31 – June 3
===========================================

Prologue: Sunday May 30
-----------------------

-  20:00 `Mongolian BBQ <http://www.djengis-khan.dk/>`_

Monday May 31 - `room U49 <http://vejviser.sdu.dk/opslag?lid=2266>`_
--------------------------------------------------------------------

-  08.55 -- 09.15 Opening, discussion of meeting program (Hans Jørgen
   Aa. Jensen)

Science with DIRAC
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  09.15 -- 09.45 On the implementation of MCSCF-srDFT models in DIRAC,
   Emmanuel Fromager (Strasbourg)
-  09.45 -- 10.15 Range-separated DFT: Group-18 dimers, Ossama Kullie
   (Strasbourg)

-  *10.15 -- 10.30 Coffee break*

-  10.30 -- 11.00 Extending DIRAC's FDE capabilities, Andre Gomes
   (Lille)
-  11.00 -- 11.30 Calculating properties for open-shell systems,
   Vladimir Malkin (Bratislava)

"Free" time
^^^^^^^^^^^

-  11.30 -- 13.30 Work on collaborative projects and *lunch*

The state of affairs
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  13.30 -- 13.45 Introduction round (everybody)
-  13.45 -- 14.15 Current structure of Dirac (Trond)
-  14.15 -- 15.00 Status of DIRAC developments in:

   -  Odense (Hans Jørgen Aa. Jensen)
   -  Strasbourg (Trond Saue)
   -  Amsterdam (Luuk Visscher)
   -  Tromsø (Kenneth Ruud)
   -  Toulouse (Timo Fleig)
   -  Heidelberg (Markus Pernpointner)
   -  Linköping (Patrick Norman)
   -  Prospects for DIRAC developments in Bratislava (Vladimir Malkin)

-  15.00 -- 15.20 Summary and mapping out of overlapping efforts (all)

xml + "Free" time
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  *15.20 -- 15.30 Coffee Break*
-  15.30 -- 16.00 xml, molecule information and Q5Cost (Kenneth Ruud)
-  16.00 -- 17.30 Collaborative projects.

-  17.30 We move our bodies (i.e. walk) to Bilka to eat dinner
-  *17.45 Dinner*

Storytelling
^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  19.00 -- 19.15 Introduction (Hans Jørgen Aa. Jensen)
-  19.15 -- 20.00 DIRAC: where we came from & a vision for where we're
   heading (Trond, Luuk and Hans Jørgen)
-  20.00 -- ??.?? General discussion of where we're heading (all)

Tuesday June 1 - `room U24 <http://vejviser.sdu.dk/opslag?lid=1082>`_
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Science with DIRAC
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  08.55 Deadline for arrival to meeting room !!! NOTE! new room.
-  09.00 -- 09.30 The XCFun DFT library. Ulf Ekström (Amsterdam)
-  09.30 -- 10.00 Status of DIRAC & OpenRSP (formerly known as
   aoresponse), Radovan Bast (Tromsø)
-  *10.00 -- 10.30 Coffee break*
-  10.30 -- 11.00 SRKB, RMB and related approaches, Stanislav Komorovsky
   (Bratislava)
-  11.00 -- 11.30 An integral package for relativistic calculations,
   Michal Repisky (Tromsø)

Technical framework for Dirac part 1
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  11.30 -- 12.00 svn setup, trac, dirlab, wiki, programming guidelines
   (Hans Jørgen Aa. Jensen)

-  *12.00 -- 13.00 Lunch*

DIRAC10 release
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  13.00 -- 13.40 Discussion of features to be released, definition of
   to-do list, release date, release message (with list of essential
   bugfixes).
-  13.40 -- 14.00 Website for licensing [radovan: can we do this on
   thursday? it will be hard for me to finish it till tuesday]

-  14:00 -- 16:00 Hands-on work on DIRAC10 & collaborative projects.
   *Coffee*

Missing features
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  16.00 -- 18.00 Decisions on implementation of some desirable but
   missing basic features:

contracted basis sets, scalar vs. 2-spinor basis, Kramers unrestricted
DFT, X2C atomic mean field, Dirac's own AMFI, better input handling, use
of higher symmetries, magnetic balance, ???

Wednesday June 2 - room U49
---------------------------

Science with DIRAC
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  08.55 Deadline for arrival to meeting room !!!
-  09.00 -- 09.30 Actinide spectroscopy, Pawel Tecmer (Amsterdam)
-  09.30 -- 10.00 The U2 bonding puzzle, Stefan Knecht (Strasbourg)
-  *10.00 -- 10.30 Coffee break*
-  10.30 -- 11.00 Mossbauer spectroscopy, Robert van Meer (Amsterdam)
-  11.00 -- 11.30 Electronic decay processes in heavy systems
   investigated by four-component propagator methods, Markus
   Pernpointner (Heidelberg)

DIRAC on tomorrows computers
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  11.30 -- 11.45 Single cpu performance: where do we spend time, Ulf
   Ekström (Amsterdam)
-  11.45 -- 12.20 MPI, Global Arrays, GPU computing, parallelization
   (discussion section)

-  *12.20 -- 13.00 Lunch* (NB! only 40 minutes)

Technical frame for Dirac part 2
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  13.00 -- 13.40 Tools & testing: test sets, trac, debuggers, etc.
   (introduction + discussion of these tools)
-  13.40 -- 14.00 Why Git is better than Subversion, Radovan Bast
   (Tromsø)
-  14.00 -- 14.30 Decisions on future technical frame for Dirac

Work to be done
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  14.30 -- 17:30 Work on the accumulated to-do list. *Coffee*

-  *19.00 Conference dinner downtown Odense*

Thursday June 3 - room U49
--------------------------

Science with DIRAC
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

-  08.55 Deadline for arrival to meeting room !!!
-  09.00 -- 09.30 Making a more efficient MRCC program, Lasse Kragh
   Sørensen (Århus)
-  09.30 -- 10.00 Noble gas matrices can change the electronic structure
   of trapped molecules: the UO2(Ng)4 case, Ivan Infante
-  *10.00 -- 10.20 Coffee break*

Conclusions and DIRAC10 Release wrap-up
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

10.20 -- 12.00 Final remarks and discussions

(Amsterdam crew leaves at 12.00)

*12:00 -- 13:00 lunch*

13:00 -- your time of departure : work on DIRAC and collaborative
projects
