Introduction
------------

The linear symmetry in DIRAC enables classification of states according
to *M*\ :sub:`*J*`\  (for atoms) and Ω (for molecules) quantum numbers;
thus there are more irreducible representations at user's disposal.

We give you some examples of employing linear symmetry in practical
calculations.
