drep output by color



The colors are determined using relative errors for numbers larger than 0.001, and absolute errors otherwise.


Only those digits that actually differ are yellow or red, the others are white.


The coloring scheme used:

1.23456789  white: difference can be neglected (less than 1e-12)
1.23456789 yellow: difference probably neglegible (less than 0.001)
1.23456789    red: probably significant difference (more than 0.001)
