Note to Dirac authors
=====================

To Dirac authors: please add your coordinates. You know best your
contributions and the amount of personal data that you wish to share
here. Don't make it too easy for harvesting spambots. Click
`here </dirac/index.php/Special:Upload>`_ to upload your photo and flag.
I got the flags from
`here <http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallery_of_sovereign_state_flags>`_.
If you don't like to the have the flags, feel free to remove them. I
think they show nicely that DIRAC is quite an international project.
